# Final exam prep

The standalone `exam-prep.Rmd` page adds 78 original multiple-choice practice questions, with six questions in each checklist area. It appears as Exam prep in the main navigation and follows the existing layout.

| Checklist area | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Quantity questions | Combien alone, combien de, à combien with parler à, combien coûte/coûtent, and retained de with avoir besoin de |
| Numbers, years, ordinals | 1997, 1,000, 27th, 30th, 200 and 200,000; cent/mille spelling qualifications |
| Time | Midi, minuit, cet après-midi, vers, 24-hour time and minuit et demi |
| Demonstratives | Ce, cet, cette, ces; vowel, h muet and h aspiré contexts |
| Imperative | Prendre, suivre, tourner, aller and continuer; tu/nous/vous and negative instructions |
| Y and en | Destinations, locations and origins; placement with an infinitive and in a negative sentence |
| Directions | Tout droit, à droite, à gauche, jusqu’à and junction/endpoint instructions |
| Regular -ir verbs | Finir, choisir, remplir, réunir, réagir and réussir with different subject pronouns |
| Interrogative words | Quel/quelle/quels; day of the week, calendar date, year and month |
| Main tenses | Present, passé composé and especially futur simple; choosing future avoir/être and distinguishing future aller from present/futur proche |
| Aller in passé composé | Être; masculine/feminine, singular/plural agreement; negation and a following infinitive |
| Time indicators | L’année passée, maintenant, bientôt, hier, la semaine prochaine; indicators do not mechanically select a tense |
| Contractions | Au, aux, du, des; jusqu’aux and origins introduced by de |

Every item has four distinct choices, one keyed answer, an English explanation and a link to an existing lesson. Distractors were reviewed to avoid treating valid alternate year readings, present forms with future meaning or regional/grammatical alternatives as errors in the same context.

Full review includes all 78 questions. Mixed sessions of 13 or 26 take one or two questions from every topic. A topic filter limits practice to its six questions. Questions and choice order are shuffled per session; stable choice IDs preserve grading after reload.

Browser storage saves the current queue, choice order, selection, checked status, position and completed results under `bloc-2-notes-exam-v1`. Existing lesson drafts, question ratings and flashcard mastery keep their existing keys and IDs. If storage is blocked, practice remains usable in memory. Invalid saved data falls back to setup.

Each answer must be selected and submitted before it counts. Feedback identifies the correct choice in text and explains it in English. Results score against the full session, list unanswered items and show a topic breakdown. Retry contains only incorrect or unsubmitted items. Starting another session clears the current exam attempt, without changing lesson or card progress.

The eight exam tests verify bank coverage/references, balanced selection, single submission, incorrect grading, pending and checked answer persistence, result/retry behavior, completion and storage failure. Existing study tests continue to verify the earlier quiz, card and inline-question tools.
