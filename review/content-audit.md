## Restored material

Every lesson now has detailed teaching sections rather than only a short overview. The pass restores full verb paradigms and spelling changes, question/preposition patterns, number spellings/readings, separately labeled example sentences, time/date/booking dialogues, pronunciation distinctions, register/regional vocabulary, error corrections and exceptions. Vocabulary includes English usage annotations rather than just two-column translations. Repeated explanations and identical examples within each lesson are consolidated. Distinct examples remain.

All 622 source self-test items have reviewed prompts/answers and consecutive source item numbers. French instructions and explanatory answers are translated into English. Exercises asking for a French answer retain French model answers. Questions with inaccurate premises/answers are retained as corrected learning questions. Supplementary drills bring the runner to 932 items. Cards contain those questions, 1,152 vocabulary entries and 419 worked examples: 2,503 cards.

## Anonymization and exclusions

Excluded material comprises names and identifying biographies, private contacts, actual personal routes, attendance/schedules, teacher handovers, messaging/download instructions, unrelated media and filler. Necessary teaching examples preserve the construction through generic people/roles and fictional scenarios. Actual routes become main/secondary streets; a personal expiry story becomes a subscription example; workbook people become pronouns or unnamed employees/families.

Public geographical names required for country/city preposition practice remain. Organization names can remain as vocabulary without attributing anyone’s employment. Exercise amounts/dates are language practice, not real bookings or current administrative requirements. Contact questions use fictional placeholders.

## Corrections and qualifications

- September 9–16: required quantity-question prepositions, multiplied/final cent, year readings, generic name/age examples, decimal/money readings. Second suggesting a pair is a tendency rather than an exceptionless limit.
- September 23–October 14: imperative irregularities, vas-y, spelling changes, vos collègues et vous → vous versus mes collègues et moi → nous, country exceptions, destination/direction, route/transport vocabulary. Dans does not require a sealed room; aller/continuer are not confined to one preposition in all uses.
- October 19–28: au printemps, date/year conventions, quel agreement, short/long time nouns, movable time expressions, indirect question order, noun/possessive agreement, regional greetings/pronunciation. Avoir lieu permits à before a clock time even though the quel jour question has no à.
- November 4: ça vous convient agrees with ça; date ranges/extensions; jeudi prochain may be ambiguous; bed dimensions are not universal.
- November 9: future tenses do not encode fixed percentages; bientôt, il y a, dans and à l’heure actuelle have flexible placement; autrefois does not absolutely force one tense; tantôt is regional/contextual; not every written r is pronounced.
- November 16: prendre → pren- for the imperfect stem; limited participle rules; pronominal agreement exceptions; transitive entrer les données → avoir versus entrer dans la salle → être; h aspiré in héros; tense recognition from the complete construction.

## Verification limits

Restoration and translation are editorial work. Automated checks verify counts, numbering, links, structure, known personal-name/contact patterns and practice behavior; they do not independently establish semantic completeness or prove every translation correct. Audio is unavailable in these exports; written listening examples are presented as text practice. No audio exercise is claimed to run.
