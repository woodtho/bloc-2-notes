## Corrections and qualifications

- The imparfait/passé composé distinction concerns viewpoint and aspect, not an absolute long-action/short-action division. Time markers help interpretation without mechanically selecting a tense.
- Subject agreement is qualified by auxiliary and construction: ordinary être, avoir with a preceding direct object, and reflexive cases.
- The extra letter in -ger spellings is e. The imparfait has mangeais but mangions/mangiez; present nous mangeons retains e before o.
- The complete present of recevoir includes nous recevons and ils/elles reçoivent. Ordinary c before r is not a soft-c rule; ç preserves /s/ before a/o/u.
- Futur proche is present aller plus an infinitive in a future construction. A present form can carry future meaning without being futur proche; the futur simple is not confined to formal speeches. Neither future form guarantees a fixed degree of certainty or distance in time.
- The greeting model uses Ça s’est bien passé ? for “Did it go well?”; the garbled source alternative is not presented as a correct model.
- Conjugueur and conjugué are distinguished; blondir is qualified; visiter/rendre visite à and faire des courses retain usage qualifications. Jusqu’à contracts specifically with le/les, rather than every following article.
